# User Manual: Maji Watch

## 1. Getting Started
Welcome to Maji Watch! This guide will help you navigate the system whether you are a concerned citizen or a county administrator.

### 1.1. Registration
1. Go to the **Register** page.
2. Fill in your full name, email, phone number, and select your county.
3. Choose a strong password.
4. Click "Create Account" to access your dashboard.

### 1.2. Logging In
1. Go to the **Login** page.
2. Enter your registered email and password.
3. Click "Login". If you forget your password, use the "Forgot Password" link.

---

## 2. For Citizens
### 2.1. Submitting a Water Issue Report
1. From your dashboard, click **"Report an Issue"**.
2. Select the issue category (e.g., Pipe Burst, Water Shortage).
3. Provide a clear title and detailed description.
4. **Location**: Use the interactive map to pin the exact location or allow the browser to use your GPS.
5. **Photos**: Upload up to 3 clear photos of the issue.
6. Select the severity level (Low to Critical).
7. Click "Submit". You will receive a unique Reference Code.

### 2.2. Tracking Your Reports
- Visit the **"My Reports"** section to see a list of all your submissions.
- Click on any report to see its current status (e.g., Verified, In Progress, Resolved) and any public comments from the administration.

### 2.3. Community Engagement
- Visit the **Public Map** to see reports from your community.
- Use the **"Upvote"** button on reports you feel are high priority. This helps the county prioritize repairs.

---

## 3. For County Admins
### 3.1. Dashboard Overview
- The **Admin Dashboard** provides real-time statistics on report volumes, categories, and resolution trends.
- Use the sidebar to switch between the Dashboard, Map View, Reports List, and Technician Management.

### 3.2. Managing Reports
1. Go to **"All Reports"**.
2. Use the filters to find specific issues (e.g., all "High" severity "Pipe Bursts" in a specific ward).
3. Click on a report to view full details, including citizen contact info and photos.
4. **Update Status**: Use the action panel to move the report through the workflow (Verify → Assign → Resolve).
5. **Add Comments**: Add internal notes for other admins or public notes for the citizen.

### 3.3. Assigning Technicians
1. While viewing a report, click **"Assign Technician"**.
2. Select an available technician from the dropdown. The system shows their current workload to help you decide.
3. The technician will be notified of their new assignment.

### 3.4. Exporting Data
- Use the **"Export CSV"** button on the reports list to download raw data for Excel.
- Use the **"Download PDF"** button to generate a formatted summary report for briefings.

---

## 4. FAQs & Troubleshooting
- **Why can't I see my location?** Ensure you have granted the browser permission to access your GPS.
- **How do I reset my password?** Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen and check your email for a reset link.
- **I am a technician but can't assign reports.** Only County Admins have permission to assign technicians.
