# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Maji Watch

## 1. Introduction
Maji Watch is a community-driven platform for monitoring water access and quality in Kenya. It aims to streamline the reporting of water infrastructure failures and improve the responsiveness of county water authorities.

## 2. Goals & Objectives
- **Goal**: Increase the efficiency of water infrastructure repairs by 30% within the first year of deployment.
- **Objective 1**: Achieve at least 5,000 active citizen users in the first six months.
- **Objective 2**: Reduce the average time from report verification to technician assignment to under 24 hours.
- **Objective 3**: Provide 100% transparency on the status of all public water issue reports.

## 3. User Personas
### 3.1. Citizen (Jane)
- **Background**: Lives in a sub-county with frequent water shortages.
- **Needs**: A simple way to report when her local borehole is broken and track when it will be fixed.
- **Pain Points**: Doesn't know who to call; feels like her complaints are ignored.

### 3.2. County Admin (Peter)
- **Background**: Works for the County Water Department.
- **Needs**: A centralized view of all reported issues to prioritize repairs and manage his team of technicians.
- **Pain Points**: Overwhelmed by phone calls and paper-based reports; lacks precise location data for repairs.

### 3.3. Field Technician (Mwangi)
- **Background**: Plumber/Electrician responsible for field repairs.
- **Needs**: Clear information on the location and nature of the issue before heading out.
- **Pain Points**: Often sent to the wrong location; doesn't have a record of past repairs at the same site.

## 4. Key Features (MVP)
1. **Interactive Map**: Visualize reports with status-colored markers.
2. **Report Submission**: Form with GPS capture, photo uploads, and severity levels.
3. **Upvoting System**: Community-driven prioritization.
4. **Admin Dashboard**: Analytics, filtering, and assignment tools.
5. **Timeline/Audit Log**: Transparent history of actions taken on a report.
6. **PDF/CSV Export**: Data reporting for official briefings.

## 5. Success Metrics (KPIs)
- **Report Volume**: Number of reports submitted per month.
- **Resolution Rate**: Percentage of reports moved to 'Resolved' status.
- **Mean Time to Resolve (MTTR)**: Average duration from 'Reported' to 'Resolved'.
- **Community Engagement**: Total number of upvotes across the platform.

## 6. Constraints & Assumptions
- **Assumption**: Users have access to smartphones with GPS capabilities.
- **Constraint**: The system must operate reliably even on slow 3G mobile networks common in rural areas.
- **Constraint**: Adherence to Kenyan government data security standards.
